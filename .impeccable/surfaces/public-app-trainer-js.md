# Winnie calendar

## Direction contract
THESIS: A shared agenda and searchable calendar history connect Winnie's scheduled life to the care already recorded. Scheduled time never silently becomes completed care.

OWN-WORLD: Extend the existing Pixelify headings, Rubik text, navy pixel controls and flat separated rows. No new decorative cards, imagery, or visual identity.

STORY: Connect the Winnie calendar once; see upcoming visits; browse historical training, vet, grooming and other entries. Exact Training w/ Will names identify Will. Matching care records are offered without duplicating them.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Plans retains Supplies and Reminders. Its middle section becomes Winnie calendar with connection status and the next three events. History’s Calendar view starts with Upcoming/Past and category controls, followed by compact chronological rows. Connection details are secondary.

FORM: Existing agenda extension, chronological rows and month groups; code-led, no concept replacement or new visual assets. Phone-first and usable at 320px in light and dark themes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
