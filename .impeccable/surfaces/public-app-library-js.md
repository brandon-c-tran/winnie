# Story and lifetime Insights

Mode: Experience for Story; Read and explore for Insights. Structural redesign within the established pixel world, code-led.

## Direction contract
THESIS: One row separates four intentions: Album, Photos, Places, Journal. Remove competing Highlights/By age/Timeline navigation. Insights explains the saved lifetime through visible charts with source access.
OWN-WORLD: Preserve Pixelify, Rubik, square controls, real photography, care colors, light/dark tokens and the room. No new asset world or tagline.
STORY: Album opens by default; photo collections are filters; month browsing is a Journal drilldown with an explicit back action. Calendar belongs to Plans. Insights defaults to whole life and retains recent tools in a disclosure.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Story header plus one navigation row leads directly to the chosen artifact. Insights opens with the date span and horizon selector, followed by a full-width bathroom-frequency plot. Chart sections then reveal daily timing, annotations, recorded nights, photo coverage and destinations.
FORM: Evidence-led visual chronology; no new concept seed because the composition is derived from the explicit navigation correction and lifetime chart request inside the pinned app world. Source values and month exploration are available beneath charts. Unknown data remain visible; no inferred health, training success, first-ever travel or total sleep claims.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
