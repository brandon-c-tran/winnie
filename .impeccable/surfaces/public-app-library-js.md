# One Story

Mode: Operate. Existing surface extension, code-led.

THESIS: Photos and care records belong to the same life. One Story destination keeps Timeline, Photos, Places and Memories together; Insights is the separate analytical destination.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve Pixelify headings, Rubik body, flat rows, pixel buttons, palette and light/dark tokens.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A single Story heading and Add photos action, four stable exploration controls, then only the current view's tools and content. Timeline shows standalone photo thumbnails alongside dated care records. Care photo attachments remain linked, individually selected keepsakes join the photo collection.

INTERACTION: Switching between views retains search and collection choices; returning from Today or Insights restores Story. Calendar is within Timeline; By age lives within Memories. Existing source-entry and date-range links open the appropriate timeline without losing the common shell.

FINISH: Mobile 390, narrow dark 320 and desktop 1440 captures; review navigation, focus, photo adding, calendar routing and no duplicated data. No new raster assets or visual-system changes.
